Privacy

This covers atheosstudios.com and the studio's private apps at home.atheosstudios.com.

This website

atheosstudios.com has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. It is served through Cloudflare, which keeps the usual short-lived request logs to run and protect the site.

The private apps

Home, Kitchen, Supercut, Budget, Smarthome and Planning are for people we invite by name. You sign in with your Google account through Cloudflare Access. Google then tells the apps your name, email address and profile photo, and nothing else.

  • Your email decides which apps you may open. Your name and photo are shown back to you in them.
  • Your data is yours. Each app shows your budget, your plans, your Google Home devices and your Kitchen week and shopping list to you and nobody else, including whoever invited you. Kitchen's recipes are the exception: they are one cookbook that everyone in Kitchen reads and adds to.
  • If you link a bank in Budget, Plaid connects to it and passes us your accounts and transactions. If you link Google Home in Smarthome, Google passes us your devices and their state.
  • All of it is kept on our own server, with a nightly backup in Google Cloud Storage that is deleted after 90 days.
  • None of it is sold, shared with advertisers, or used for anything but running the apps for you.

Removing your access or your data

Email josh.wollberg@gmail.com and we remove your access. Ask, and we delete your data in every app at the same time: your budget and bank link, your plans, your Google Home link, and your meal plans and lists. Recipes you added stay in the shared cookbook unless you ask for those to go too. Copies in the backups are gone within 90 days. You can also remove the app from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
